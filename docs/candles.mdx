---
title: 'Index and rate candles'
description: 'OHLC candles over published index and reference-rate levels'
---

Candles bucket the published index or rate level series into open, high, low, and close. They are calculated levels, not venue trades, so a candle has no bid, ask, or volume.

`timestamp` is the end of the bucket, in UTC, the same convention as market candles. `1min`, `5min`, and `1hour` buckets align to UTC. `1d` buckets use US Eastern midnights (the same day boundary as price history) and `timestamp` is the following Eastern midnight. A bucket with no level is omitted.

Official daily values (`/daily-values`) are a separate series and are not changed.

Price history stamps the start of a bucket and returns that bucket's last level. A candle stamps the end of the bucket. One hourly level at `2026-10-26T12:00:00Z` is the candle that ends at `2026-10-26T13:00:00Z`.

## Routes

| Tier | Index | Reference rate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Public, 15-minute delayed, no credential | `GET /api/v1/public/indices/{id}/candles` | `GET /api/v1/public/rates/{id}/candles` |
| Authenticated, same credential as price history | `GET /api/v1/indices/{id}/candles` | `GET /api/v1/rates/{id}/candles` |

Public `interval` is `1hour` (30-day window, the default) or `1d` (90-day window). `daily` and `1day` are aliases of `1d`.

Authenticated `interval` is `1min`, `5min` (the default), `1hour`, or `1d`, with the same aliases. Realtime history uses the price route's plan gate. A Pro or Premium refusal is returned unchanged.

Authenticated candles are built from the finest published level series that fits the window (`1min`, then `5min`, then `1hour`), and never from a series coarser than the candle. The default window is 7 days for `1min` and `5min`, 30 days for `1hour`, and 90 days for `1d`.

`from` is an inclusive lower bound on the candle timestamp. `to` is exclusive. A date-only `from` is UTC midnight. A date-only `to` covers that whole UTC day (exclusive at the next UTC midnight). `page` defaults to 1. `per_page` defaults to 100 and accepts 1 through 500. `order` is `asc` or `desc` (default `desc`).

On the public tier, a `1d` candle inside the last 30 days is the open, high, low, and close of the hourly levels. An older day in the 90-day window has only the published daily level, so open, high, and low equal that close.

## Example

`GET /api/v1/public/indices/{id}/candles?interval=1hour&order=desc`

Levels 10, 14, and 9 in `[12:00, 13:00)`, then 11 and 12 in `[13:00, 14:00)`, come back as:

```json
{
  "data": [
    {
      "timestamp": "2026-10-26T14:00:00Z",
      "open": 11,
      "high": 12,
      "low": 11,
      "close": 12
    },
    {
      "timestamp": "2026-10-26T13:00:00Z",
      "open": 10,
      "high": 14,
      "low": 9,
      "close": 9
    }
  ],
  "meta": {
    "total": 2,
    "page": 1,
    "per_page": 100,
    "total_pages": 1,
    "has_next": false,
    "has_prev": false
  }
}
```
